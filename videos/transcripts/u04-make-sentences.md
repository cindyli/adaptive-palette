# U4 · Make Sentences

- For: AAC users
- After watching, you can: turn your message into full sentences, choose one to say it, type your own, and start
  your next message
- Watch first: Word Suggestions
- Length: about 3½ minutes
- Made from: the app, with one About Me note saved in advance ("has a brother named Sam"), prepared AI answers, and
  the app's voice recorded

## Why

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Title card: "Make Sentences" | Make Sentences. |
| 2 | The message *I* *want* *walk* *brother*, with a box around it | Your message has the key words: I want walk brother. **Make Sentences** turns key words into full sentences, the way you would say them. |

## Make sentences

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | A box around **Make Sentences**, to the right of the message; the pointer chooses it | To try it, choose **Make Sentences**, to the right of your message. |
| 4 | **⏳ Making sentences…** above two empty places | The screen says: making sentences. The AI is writing them for you. On your computer, this can take a few seconds. |
| 5 | **Choose a sentence** over the palette; a box around the first sentence, "I want to go for a walk with Sam." | The sentences cover the palette, under the words **Choose a sentence**. The first one says: I want to go for a walk with Sam. |
| 6 | A box around the second sentence, "Can Sam and I go for a walk?" | The second one says: Can Sam and I go for a walk? |
| 7 | A box around the Bliss symbols of the first sentence | Each sentence is written in Bliss symbols, with the words under them, so you can read it in Bliss. |
| 8 | An arrow to the **AI** mark in the corner of a sentence | The **AI** mark in the corner shows the AI wrote this sentence. Check that it says what you mean. |
| 9 | A box around "Sam" in the first sentence | The AI even knows your brother's name, Sam, because **About Me** tells it. The About Me video shows how. |

## Change your message

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | A box around **Edit message** | To change your message, choose **Edit message**. It closes the sentences and takes you back to your message. |
| 15 | The pointer chooses **Delete** on the command bar; the box **Change your message?** opens | If you change your message while the sentences show, for example with **Delete**, a box asks: **Change your message?** **Changing your message will remove the sentences. Change anyway?** |
| 16 | A box around **Change anyway** and **Keep sentences**; the pointer chooses **Keep sentences** | **Change anyway** removes the sentences and changes your message. **Keep sentences** keeps them, and your message stays as it was. |

## Done

| # | On screen | Narration |
