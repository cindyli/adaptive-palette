# S1 · Installing the Desktop Version

- For: support people
- After watching, you can: choose the right version, install Ollama and Adaptive Palette on a Mac or a Windows
  computer, start the app, and stop it
- Watch first: nothing; this is the first support video
- Length: about 5 minutes
- Made from: screenshots of the Mac and Windows steps, then the app

## Two versions

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Title card: "Installing the Desktop Version" | Installing the desktop version. |
| 2 | The public website, with **Trial version: no AI, nothing saved** in its top bar | The public website lets anyone try Adaptive Palette without installing anything. Its top bar says: **Trial version: no AI, nothing saved**. |
| 3 | The desktop version, with its AI features | For everyday use, install the desktop version. Its AI runs on this computer, and it saves the person's messages so the AI can fit them. Nothing they write leaves the computer. |

## Before you start

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | A slide: "Windows, or macOS 13.5 or newer. With the AI: 16 GB of memory, 10 GB of free disk space. Without the AI: 1 GB of free disk space." | It runs on Windows, or on macOS 13.5 or newer. ## Download Adaptive Palette

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | Screenshot: the newest release on the releases page, with **Assets** open, and "AdaptivePalette-macos.zip" and "AdaptivePalette-windows.zip" | Open the releases page, linked in the description, and open the newest release. ## On a Mac

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | Screenshot: **Adaptive Palette** dragged from the zip to the Applications folder | Open the zip, and drag **Adaptive Palette** to your Applications folder or your Desktop. |
| 11 | Screenshot: the warning that Apple cannot check the app, with **Done** | The first time you open it, a warning says Apple cannot check the app for malicious software. That is because the app is not signed with a paid certificate yet. Choose **Done**. |
| 12 | Screenshots: the Apple menu, **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security** in the side menu | Open the Apple menu in the top left corner, and choose **System Settings**. Then choose **Privacy & Security** in the side menu. |
| 13 | Screenshot: the **Security** section, with **Open Anyway** next to the message about the app | Scroll down to the **Security** section, and choose **Open Anyway** next to the message about the app. |
| 14 | Screenshot: the password box, with **OK** | Type your Mac login password, and choose **OK**. You only have to do this once. |

## On Windows

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | Screenshot: the **AdaptivePalette** folder dragged from the zip to the Desktop | Open the zip, and drag the **AdaptivePalette** folder to where you want to keep it, such as your Desktop. The app is the whole folder, so keep it together. |
| 16 | Screenshot: the **AdaptivePalette** file with two gears, inside the folder | The **AdaptivePalette** file inside it, with the two gears, starts the app. You can make a shortcut to it on your Desktop. |
| 17 | Screenshots: the blue **Windows protected your PC** box, **More info**, then **Run anyway** | The first time, a blue box says: **Windows protected your PC**. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You only have to do this once. |

## Start and stop

| # | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | The web browser opening at localhost:3210, with the palette | Start the app. Your usual web browser opens, with the palette in it. |
| 19 | The setup box | The first time, a box may say the AI features are not ready yet. The next video shows what to do. |
| 20 | The browser tab closing, then the app opened again | Closing the browser tab does not stop the app. It keeps running quietly, and opening the app again brings the browser straight back to it. |
| 21 | Screenshots: the black console window on Windows; a Mac restarting | To stop it on Windows, close the black window that opened when the app started. On a Mac, it stops when you restart or shut down the computer. |
| 22 | **Adjust Settings**, with a box around **Erase all app data and quit** | **Erase all app data and quit**, in **Adjust Settings**, is not a quit button. It deletes everything the person has saved, and it cannot be undone. |
