A short overview of Adaptive Palette, a communication board that uses Bliss symbols, pictures, or both. Its AI adapts the board to you, and runs on your own computer, so your messages stay private.

In this video:
• Changing a Bliss word with indicators and modifiers, with AI working out the new word
• Building a new word from symbols you know, with AI suggesting names for it
• Word suggestions for your next word
• Turning a few symbols into full sentences
• Setting the style of your sentences, such as happy, polite or urgent
• How Adaptive Palette learns about you, and how you decide what it keeps

Try it online: https://adaptive-palette.pages.dev/
AI features need the desktop version, which runs the AI on your own computer so your messages stay private.
Install the desktop version: https://github.com/inclusive-design/adaptive-palette/blob/main/docs/Deployment.md
