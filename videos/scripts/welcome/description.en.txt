A short tour of Adaptive Palette, a Bliss symbol communication board that explores how AI can help people who use AAC communicate. Its AI suggests words, builds full sentences, and learns what matters to you.

In this video:
• How AI helps you communicate
• Where your message appears
• Opening a folder of symbols and adding symbols to your message
• Word suggestions for your next word
• The buttons that move through your message, delete symbols and add more meaning
• Turning your message into full sentences, and choosing one to say

Buttons and folders can also show pictures in place of Bliss symbols.

Try it online: https://adaptive-palette.pages.dev/
AI features need the desktop version, which runs the AI on your own computer so your messages stay private.
Install the desktop version: https://github.com/inclusive-design/adaptive-palette/blob/main/docs/Deployment.md
