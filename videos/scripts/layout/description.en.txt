A welcome to Adaptive Palette, a communication board that uses Bliss symbols, pictures, or both, and a tour of its layout. Its AI adapts the board to you, and runs on your own computer, so your messages stay private.

In this video:
• What the AI in Adaptive Palette does for you
• How vocabulary is grouped into folders
• The message window, and the buttons around it
• Word suggestions, and the row of editing cells
• Editing a message: moving the cursor, adding words, deleting words

Try it online: https://adaptive-palette.pages.dev/
AI features need the desktop version, which runs the AI on your own computer so your messages stay private.
Install the desktop version: https://github.com/inclusive-design/adaptive-palette/blob/main/docs/Deployment.md
